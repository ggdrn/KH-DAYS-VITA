# khdays-vita v0.8.0

Install `khdays-vita-0.8.0.vpk` over the previous version with VitaShell. Saves and settings are
kept.

## New

- **Screen layout "left"**: one screen 4:3 on the left over the full height, the other small
  beside it (`layout = left` / `left_bottom`; touch the small one to swap).
- **Widescreen HUD at the edges** (`hud = 2`): the 2D stays 4:3 while the commands, HP and chain
  sit at the display's edges.
- **60 fps falls back to a steady 30** for a while when the display cannot keep up, instead of
  running unevenly, then tries 60 again.
- **Lighter dual-3D scenes**: scenes with 3D on both screens lower the 3D resolution while they
  fall behind.

## Experimental

- **Single screen** (port menu, SCREEN tab):
  - Before the game, the title's menu is shown over a picture. A build can pack one from
    `assets/titlescreen/bg.png`; otherwise `ux0:data/khdays/menu_bg.png` (or `.jpg`) is used.
  - Dialogue scenes whose bottom screen stays black are shown as the top screen alone, in
    widescreen. Anything on the bottom screen brings your usual layout back.
  - World / mission selection: the world fills the display, with the mission list beside it.
- **Partner swap** (port menu, new PARTNER tab; set it outside a mission):
  - In a story mission, touch the partner's HUD to play as the partner. The AI plays Roxas.
  - The partner keeps its own deck and HP. It gets the stats of Roxas's weapon and his
    abilities (dodge roll, glide, scan...).
  - Roxas on the AI fights with the partner's weapon stats. Options allow him to use his own
    items and magic (off by default).

## Fixed

- **Mission 54 crash** on entering the Dual Blade's arena. The same bug was also fixed in the
  Dual Blade, Blitz Spear, Stalwart Blade, Chill Ripper and Heat Saber.
- **The Possessor's white flash** when it dies is gone.
- **Loud noise in the camp menu's audio.**
- **Garbled or stale sound** in scenes that load another sound archive.
- **The right-stick camera** no longer swings round by itself during a sideways walk
  (`camera_follow = 0`).
- **30 fps pacing**: each game frame is shown as soon as it is ready.

## Known issues

- Partner swap is experimental: the partner's weapon model, combos and animations stay its own,
  and the HUD's large and small bars do not swap.
- The busiest missions at 4x can still drop below 60 fps. Keep `dynamic_res = 1` or use
  `render_scale = 3`.
- `debug = 1` measures the GPU once a second, which costs a frame each time: keep it at 0 to
  play.
- Multiplayer is not available.
