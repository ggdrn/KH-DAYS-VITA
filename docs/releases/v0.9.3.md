# khdays-vita v0.9.3

Install `khdays-vita-0.9.3.vpk` over the previous version with VitaShell. Saves and settings are
kept.

## Fixed

- The post-credits scene opens the correct save screen and writes the cleared-game save. Theater
  Mode and the Zero Gear unlock are available after completing the story.
- Resuming from the pause menu during the final Riku battle no longer resets its dual-screen
  presentation, which could leave most of the fight black.
- In widescreen with the `hud = 1` 4:3 HUD, opening the shortcut with L no longer flashes a
  corrupted top section of the interface. The transition now finishes its HUD picture before it
  is displayed.
- Fixed the temporary stretched geometry on Darkside in Holo-Mission **Two Keys** in widescreen.
  The 3D projection is now fixed for a complete frame, so a display-screen switch cannot apply
  different horizontal scales to adjacent model vertices. Position tests used by the target marker
  use that same frame projection.

## Known issues

- Partner swap is experimental: the partner's weapon model, combos and animations stay its own.
- Multiplayer is not available.
