# khdays-vita v0.7.0

Install `khdays-vita-0.7.0.vpk` over the previous version with VitaShell. Saves and settings are
kept. The first boot takes a few seconds longer: the shaders are compiled again.

## Changed

- **Fights with 3D on both screens** (Sora on the bottom screen in Olympus Coliseum): with the
  single screen on, both screens are shown for the first 10 seconds and the single screen comes
  back after. The scenery no longer shakes and the 3D no longer jumps between the screens, and
  the fight runs at full speed (it dropped to about 25-40 fps).
- **60 fps (experimental) holds its rate in busy scenes.** The 3D is drawn at a lower
  resolution (down to 2x) while the GPU cannot keep up, and back at the configured one when it
  can. The screen's composition takes less of the GPU.
- **Movies play at a steady 30 fps**, in the 60 fps mode too (they played at about 50).
- **Sound uses about 40% less CPU**: it is mixed at the DS's own rate (32 kHz), the Vita
  converting it.

## Fixed

- Hitches when a new area or mission loads: the cartridge dump is read through a 16 MB cache
  that reads ahead, instead of stopping the game for each piece.
- The pause menu stuttered for a moment when opened in the 60 fps mode.

## Known issues

- The busiest missions can still slow down for a moment: the DS game code itself is near the
  limit of a Vita core there.
- In 60 fps mode, a few frames in crowded fights still come late, and 2D backgrounds that follow
  the camera move at 30 fps.
- `debug = 1` in `config.ini` measures the GPU once a second, which costs a frame each time:
  keep it at 0 to play.
- Multiplayer is not available.
