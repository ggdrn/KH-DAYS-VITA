# khdays-vita v0.4.0

Install `khdays-vita-0.4.0.vpk` over the previous version with VitaShell. Saves and settings are
kept.

## New

- **Single screen (beta)**: in missions, the bottom screen's map, target, mission gauge and
  INFORMATION bar are shown as panels over the top screen. They appear when they change, while
  the game is paused, or while the camera is zoomed in on the player (Select).
- **HUD size** (60–100%): shrinks the command menu, HP gauges and chain counter; dialogue
  boxes, menus and notices keep their size.
- **Language**: English, French, German, Italian or Spanish (takes effect on restart).
- **R one-click lock-on**: one press locks on, another releases. While it is on, R no longer
  recenters the camera.

## Changed

- **30 fps by default.** 60 fps is now marked experimental in the port menu: the scenery can
  shake while the camera turns, and some effects can glitch.
- Quality presets no longer turn 60 fps on.

## Fixed

- Missing pieces in characters' hair (Larxene, Marluxia, Xion and others).
- Black box in the corner during field conversations.
- Notices ("BACKPACK FULL", "OBTAINED", the item bar) broken by the HUD size.
- Crash at boot without kubridge installed.

## Performance

- The FPS counter cost about 10 ms per frame; it is now almost free.
- At 30 fps, the 2D is drawn across two frames instead of holding up the display.
- Faster 3D on the GPU (opaque polygons) and less CPU time spent uploading the screens.
- Fewer missed frames: the port's threads no longer compete with the system's display thread.

## Known issues

- A white flash can appear when some enemies are defeated.
- Short hitches when entering a new area or when many enemies appear (texture loading).
- Multiplayer is not available.
