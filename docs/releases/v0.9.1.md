# khdays-vita v0.9.1

Install `khdays-vita-0.9.1.vpk` over the previous version with VitaShell. Saves and settings are
kept.

## Fixed

- The post-credits scene now opens the correct save screen and writes the cleared-game save.
  Theater Mode and the Zero Gear unlock are available after completing the story.
- Resuming from the pause menu during the final Riku battle no longer resets its dual-screen
  presentation, which could leave most of the fight black.

## Known issues

- Partner swap is experimental: the partner's weapon model, combos and animations stay its own.
- Multiplayer is not available.
