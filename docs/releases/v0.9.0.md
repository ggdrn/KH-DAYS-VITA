# khdays-vita v0.9.0

Install `khdays-vita-0.9.0.vpk` over the previous version with VitaShell. Saves and settings are
kept.

## New

- **Cutscenes with 3D on both screens** run at full speed: each screen keeps its own picture
  instead of a GPU copy at every frame. The 3D stays at `render_scale`, and the dialogue box no
  longer shakes.
- **60 fps runs as fast as the GPU allows** (40-60 in heavy fights) instead of falling back to a
  steady 30.
- **Mission Mode enemy HP**: Original (3x), 2.5x, 2x, 1.5x or Story.

## Experimental

- **Single screen**:
  - Out of the field, scenes show the top screen alone in widescreen. When the bottom screen
    plays something (a movie, Sora's 3D), it fades in centred over it at 70% size and half
    opacity, and fades out when it ends.
  - In the field, a new HUD laid out for one screen: mission panel and hearts top left, enemy
    health, map and sigils top right.
  - More selection pages (mission confirmation, day list) use the world-and-list layout.
- **Widescreen HUD at the edges** (`hud = 2`): item notices now move to the edges too.

## Fixed

- **Crash on day 255** (the scene that seats the party).

## Known issues

- **60 fps bottleneck in single screen mode.** The new single screen compositing (the field HUD
  pieces, the panels and the 2D smoothing over the whole display) is heavy on the GPU: in busy
  areas the 60 fps mode often stays around 30-40. Two-screen layouts reach 60 more easily;
  `filter_2d = 0` or `fps = 30` also help.
- Partner swap is experimental: the partner's weapon model, combos and animations stay its own.
- `debug = 1` measures the GPU once a second, which costs a frame each time: keep it at 0 to
  play.
- Multiplayer is not available.
