# khdays-vita v0.5.0

Install `khdays-vita-0.5.0.vpk` over the previous version with VitaShell. Saves and settings are
kept; the new options start at their defaults.

## New

- **PlayStation buttons on screen.** The game's A/B/X/Y icons are drawn as the Vita's Circle,
  Cross, Triangle and Square: in all text (tutorials, hints, menus), the battle HUD (shortcut
  buttons, the combo prompt over the player), the camp menu, the panel screen and its help
  lines. Port menu → System → **Button icons** (PS Vita / DS; takes effect on restart).
- **Cross confirms, Circle cancels** (default). The DS's A is on Cross and B on Circle, in the
  menus and in the field; the on-screen icons follow. Port menu → System → **Confirm button**
  (Cross / Circle) switches back to the DS layout (A on Circle).
- **Switch targets with the right stick.** While locked on, flick the right stick left or right
  to lock on to the nearest enemy on that side; hold it to keep switching. The camera still
  tilts with the stick's vertical axis.
- **Mission balance.** In Mission Mode, played solo, enemies have three times the HP and hit
  harder, since the mode is made for up to four players. Port menu → System → **Mission
  balance** → Story gives them the story's HP, and the damage you take follows your save's
  difficulty (Standard for a guest).

## Changed

- **30 fps is now a steady 30 fps.** The screen used to refresh at 60 and show each game frame
  twice, which frame counters reported as dropping below 60. It now refreshes at exactly 30.
- **60 fps (experimental) no longer shakes.** The frames in between are built model by model
  and timed by the screen's own refresh: the floor, the sky, map pieces near the camera and
  scrolling textures (water, sky) move smoothly.

## Fixed

- Cutscenes could freeze for a moment while the game was busy (reported on Reddit).
- The white flash when some enemies are defeated (the Possessor's burst): overlapping
  translucent effects no longer add up into a white blot.

## Performance

- Sound takes half the CPU it did (about 12% of a core in the field, from 25%): instrument
  samples are decoded once instead of for every note.
- During movies the game's core goes from 100% to about 10% (the players sleep between frames
  instead of waiting busily), and the movie decoder is faster.
- New areas load their textures with less work on the display thread.

## Known issues

- Short hitches when entering a new area (many textures at once).
- Movies play at about 50 fps on the screen.
- In 60 fps mode, 2D backgrounds that follow the camera still move at 30 fps.
- Some DS button graphics may remain in screens not checked yet. Please report them (a frame
  dump helps: `debug = 1` in `config.ini`, then L+R+Triangle on that screen).
- Multiplayer is not available.
